Privacy Policy

Timeless Creator Discovery, last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

Timeless Creator Discovery is an internal tool operated by Timeless s.r.l. ("Timeless Club"). It is used only by Timeless Club staff to find and evaluate Instagram and Facebook creators for brand partnerships. Contact: thetimelessclubteam@gmail.com.

What data the tool accesses

When a Timeless Club staff member signs in with Facebook, the tool requests access to the Timeless Club Facebook Page and its connected Instagram business account. Through Meta's Creator Marketplace API it then reads creator information that Meta makes available to brands: username, biography, country, profile picture, public contact email, portfolio link, badges, audience and performance insights, and recent content.

How the data is used

The data is used only to search for creators, review their profiles and keep an internal shortlist with notes for possible collaborations. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising targeting or profiling outside this purpose.

Where the data is stored

The tool has no server and no database. Search results are shown in the browser and are not kept. The access token and the shortlist of saved creators (with status and notes) are stored only in the local storage of the staff member's own browser. Exports are CSV files downloaded to the staff member's device.

Data deletion

Staff can remove a saved creator at any time from the tool, and "Sign out" (Esci) deletes the access token from the browser. Creators or other individuals who want their data removed from our shortlists or exports can write to thetimelessclubteam@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days. Access granted to the app can also be revoked at any time from Facebook settings, under Business Integrations.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
